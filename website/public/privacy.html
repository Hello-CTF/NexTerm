NexTerm 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日 · 适用版本：NexTerm 全平台（桌面端、iOS、Android、服务端）

NexTerm 是一个以"本地优先、自托管"为原则的远程终端与运维工具。本政策说明 App 会处理哪些数据、存在哪里。简短版：你的服务器凭据和终端数据默认只保存在你自己的设备上；我们运营方无法读取你的任何凭据或会话内容。

1. 我们处理的数据及用途

  • 账号信息（服务端模式）：用户名、加密后的凭据。用于登录与多设备同步。
  • 资产与凭据：你添加的 SSH/WinRM 主机、保存的密码与私钥。仅用于建立你发起的连接。
  • 终端历史：会话的输出与键盘输入记录，用于回放与搜索功能。注意：其中可能包含你输入过的密码或令牌。
  • 设备信息（设备管理功能）：设备名、系统版本、在线状态，用于远程打开设备终端。

2. 数据存储位置

  • 本地优先：资产、凭据、终端历史默认只存储在你当前使用的设备上，受系统钥匙串（macOS/iOS）或 DPAPI（Windows）或你设置的保护密码加密保护。
  • 端到端加密同步：只有你主动登录账号并开启同步后，资产、分组、片段、凭据和可选配置才会同步；同步内容端到端加密，服务端只存密文，无法解密。
  • 终端历史不同步：终端历史默认仅本地保存，不参与同步；你可以对单条记录单独开启端到端加密同步，也可以随时逐条删除。
  • 自托管：服务端由你或你的团队自行部署，数据保存在你自己的机器或数据库（SQLite / PostgreSQL / MySQL / MariaDB）中。

3. 生物识别

Face ID / 指纹仅用于在本机解锁凭据库，验证结果不会离开设备，我们也不会收集任何生物特征数据。

4. AI 助手功能

AI 功能由你自行配置 OpenAI 兼容接口（可以是本地模型或任何第三方端点）。你发送给 AI 的终端内容、文件内容会直接传输到你所配置的端点，其隐私由该端点的运营方负责，我们不中转、不存储这些内容。

5. 分享链接

你可以主动把设备或会话生成限时公开链接。链接默认只读，只有显式授权才允许读写，可随时吊销。获得链接的人在有效期内无需登录即可访问，请仅授予可信对象。

6. 第三方共享

我们不嵌入任何第三方分析、广告或追踪 SDK，不向任何第三方出售或共享你的数据。除你自建的 AI 端点和自托管服务端外，数据不会离开你的设备。

7. 数据删除

你可以随时在 App 内删除单条终端历史、资产或凭据；卸载 App 会删除本地数据；自托管服务端的数据由你自行管理，删除数据目录即可完全清除。

8. 政策更新与联系

政策更新会在本页面发布。问题反馈请到 GitHub Issues。

NexTerm Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · Applies to NexTerm on all platforms (desktop, iOS, Android, server)

NexTerm is a remote terminal and operations tool built on local-first, self-hosted principles. Short version: your server credentials and terminal data stay on your own devices by default, and we, the operator, cannot read any of your credentials or session content.

1. Data We Process and Why

  • Account data (server mode): username and encrypted credentials, used for sign-in and multi-device sync.
  • Assets and credentials: SSH/WinRM hosts you add, saved passwords and private keys, used only to establish connections you initiate.
  • Terminal history: recordings of session output and keystrokes, used for replay and search. Note: these may include passwords or tokens you typed.
  • Device information (device management): device name, OS version, and online status, used to open remote terminals on enrolled devices.

2. Where Data Is Stored

  • Local-first: assets, credentials, and terminal history are stored only on the device you use, protected by the OS keychain (macOS/iOS), DPAPI (Windows), or a protection password you set.
  • End-to-end encrypted sync: only after you sign in and enable sync are assets, groups, snippets, credentials, and optional settings synchronized. Synced content is end-to-end encrypted; the server stores ciphertext only and cannot decrypt it.
  • Terminal history is not synced: it stays on your device by default; you may enable end-to-end encrypted sync per entry or delete any entry at any time.
  • Self-hosted: the server is deployed by you or your team; data lives on your own machines or databases (SQLite / PostgreSQL / MySQL / MariaDB).

3. Biometrics

Face ID / Touch ID is used solely to unlock the local credential vault. Biometric data never leaves your device, and we collect none.

4. AI Assistant

AI features run against an OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configure yourself (a local model or any third-party endpoint). Terminal and file content you send to the AI goes directly to that endpoint; its privacy is governed by that endpoint's operator. We do not relay or store this content.

5. Sharing Links

You may create time-limited public links to devices or sessions. Links are read-only by default; read-write access requires explicit authorization and links can be revoked at any time. Anyone with the link can access it within its validity period without signing in — share only with trusted parties.

6. Third Parties

We embed no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and we never sell or share your data. Apart from the AI endpoint you configure and your self-hosted server, your data does not leave your devices.

7. Deleting Your Data

You can delete individual terminal history entries, assets, or credentials in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app removes local data. Self-hosted server data is managed by you; deleting its data directory erases it completely.

8. Updates and Contact

Updates to this policy are posted on this page. Questions and feedback: GitHub Issues.
