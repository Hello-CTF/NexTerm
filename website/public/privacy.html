NexTerm 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 19 日 · 适用版本：NexTerm 全平台（桌面端、iOS、Android、服务端）

NexTerm 是一个本地优先、可自托管的远程终端与运维工具。本政策说明应用会处理哪些数据、存在哪里。桌面和移动端的数据可保存在当前设备；使用浏览器模式时，你部署的服务端会处理连接、工作区和账号数据。项目维护者不代你托管服务端。

1. 我们处理的数据及用途

  • 账号信息（服务端模式）：用户名、密码摘要和登录会话，用于身份验证与多设备同步。
  • 资产与凭据：你添加的 SSH/WinRM 主机、保存的密码与私钥。仅用于建立你发起的连接。
  • 终端历史：会话的输出与键盘输入记录，用于回放与搜索功能。注意：其中可能包含你输入过的密码或令牌。
  • 设备信息（设备管理功能）：设备名、系统版本、在线状态，用于远程打开设备终端。

2. 数据存储位置

  • 本地数据：桌面和移动端在应用数据目录保存资产、设置、终端历史和 AI 对话。凭据库可使用系统保护或你设置的保护密码；终端历史可能含敏感输入，请保护应用数据目录和备份。
  • 端到端加密同步：使用同步功能时，资产、分组、片段、凭据和可选配置作为加密同步对象传输；服务端无法解密这些同步对象。
  • 终端历史：历史记录默认不参与跨设备同步；你可以对单条记录单独开启端到端加密同步，也可以逐条删除。浏览器模式的会话和历史由你部署的服务端处理和保存。
  • 自托管服务端：服务端由你或你的团队部署，账号、工作区、会话及 AI 对话等数据保存在其数据目录或数据库（SQLite / PostgreSQL / MySQL / MariaDB）中。服务端管理员应控制访问权限和备份。

3. 生物识别

Face ID / 指纹仅用于在本机解锁凭据库，验证结果不会离开设备，我们也不会收集任何生物特征数据。

4. AI 助手功能

AI 功能使用你配置的 OpenAI 兼容接口，可以是本地模型或第三方端点。请求由运行 AI 功能的 NexTerm 实例发送：桌面端由本机发送，浏览器模式由你部署的服务端发送。发送给模型的对话、终端或文件内容受该端点的隐私规则约束；AI 对话和运行记录也会保存在相应 NexTerm 实例的数据存储中。

5. 分享链接

你可以主动把设备或会话生成限时公开链接。链接默认只读，只有显式授权才允许读写，可随时吊销。获得链接的人在有效期内无需登录即可访问，请仅授予可信对象。

6. 第三方共享

本官网不加载第三方分析、广告或追踪脚本，项目维护者不出售用户数据。你主动使用远程连接、同步、AI 或更新功能时，相关请求会发送至相应的主机或服务。

7. 数据删除

你可以在应用内删除单条终端历史、资产或凭据。卸载应用未必会删除其数据目录；如需清除本地数据，请按所在平台删除应用数据目录及备份。自托管服务端还需清理其数据目录、所用数据库及备份。

8. 政策更新与联系

政策更新会在本页面发布。问题反馈请到 GitHub Issues。

NexTerm Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026 · Applies to NexTerm on all platforms (desktop, iOS, Android, server)

NexTerm is a local-first, self-hostable remote terminal and operations tool. This policy explains what the app processes and where it is stored. Desktop and mobile data can stay on the current device; in browser mode, the server you deploy processes connections, workspaces, and account data. The project maintainers do not host your server for you.

1. Data We Process and Why

  • Account data (server mode): username, password hash, and login sessions, used for authentication and multi-device sync.
  • Assets and credentials: SSH/WinRM hosts you add, saved passwords and private keys, used only to establish connections you initiate.
  • Terminal history: recordings of session output and keystrokes, used for replay and search. Note: these may include passwords or tokens you typed.
  • Device information (device management): device name, OS version, and online status, used to open remote terminals on enrolled devices.

2. Where Data Is Stored

  • Local data: desktop and mobile apps store assets, settings, terminal history, and AI conversations in the app data directory. The credential vault can use OS protection or a protection password you set. Terminal history may contain sensitive input; protect the app data directory and its backups.
  • End-to-end encrypted sync: when you use sync, assets, groups, snippets, credentials, and optional settings are transmitted as encrypted sync objects. The server cannot decrypt those sync objects.
  • Terminal history: recordings are excluded from cross-device sync by default. You may enable end-to-end encrypted sync per entry or delete entries individually. In browser mode, sessions and history are processed and stored by the server you deploy.
  • Self-hosted server: you or your team deploy the server. Accounts, workspaces, sessions, AI conversations, and related data live in its data directory or database (SQLite / PostgreSQL / MySQL / MariaDB). Server administrators should control access and backups.

3. Biometrics

Face ID / Touch ID is used solely to unlock the local credential vault. Biometric data never leaves your device, and we collect none.

4. AI Assistant

AI features use an OpenAI-compatible endpoint you configure, whether a local model or a third-party endpoint. Requests are sent by the NexTerm instance running AI: your device in desktop mode, or your deployed server in browser mode. Conversations, terminal output, and file content sent to a model are subject to that endpoint's privacy rules. AI conversations and run records are also stored by the corresponding NexTerm instance.

5. Sharing Links

You may create time-limited public links to devices or sessions. Links are read-only by default; read-write access requires explicit authorization and links can be revoked at any time. Anyone with the link can access it within its validity period without signing in — share only with trusted parties.

6. Third Parties

This website loads no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. The project maintainers do not sell user data. When you use remote connections, sync, AI, or updates, related requests go to the respective hosts or services.

7. Deleting Your Data

You can delete individual terminal history entries, assets, or credentials in the app. Uninstalling the app may leave its data directory behind; to remove local data, delete that directory and its backups according to your platform. For a self-hosted server, also remove its data directory, database, and backups.

8. Updates and Contact

Updates to this policy are posted on this page. Questions and feedback: GitHub Issues.
